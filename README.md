### 03 — [Retail Sales & Profit Dashboard](03-retail-sales-profit-dashboard)
![Retail Sales & Profit Dashboard](03-retail-sales-profit-dashboard/images/dashboard.png)

A full retail analytics project: 12 monthly CSV files merged and cleaned into 1,200 orders (₹5.43M sales, ₹1.21M profit), then analysed to find where profit is made and lost.

**Work performed:**
- Power Query merge of 12 monthly CSV files
- Fixing 4 planted data issues (spacing and capitalisation, `Yes/No` vs `Y/N`, duplicate rows)
- Reconciliation of row counts and totals (1,210 → 1,200 rows)
- KPIs using `SUMIFS`, `COUNTIFS`, `SUMPRODUCT`
- Product ABC analysis (13 products make 79% of sales)
- Customer RFM segmentation (67 Champions make 63% of sales)
- Discount analysis (orders discounted above 20% lose money)
- Pivot-style summaries and a one-page dashboard with KPI cards, charts and filters
- Five business insights with recommendations and a one-page PDF summary
- Python script to verify the cleaning against control totals

**Key skills:** `Excel` `Power Query` `SUMPRODUCT` `INDEX/MATCH` `ABC Analysis` `RFM Analysis` `Dashboard Design` `Business Insights`
